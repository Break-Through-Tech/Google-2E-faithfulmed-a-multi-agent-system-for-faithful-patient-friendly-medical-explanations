Ramsay Hunt syndrome type II.

Zhu S(1), Pyatkevich Y.

Author information:
(1)From Boston University School of Medicine, Boston University Medical Center, 
MA.

A 57-year-old man developed 3 days of left facial pain and swelling with 
left-sided hearing loss followed by a painful, unilateral, erythematous, and 
vesicular rash on the left anterior two-thirds of the tongue, external auditory 
canal, lip, and face typical of varicella-zoster virus reactivation (figure). 
Reactivation in the geniculate ganglion or facial nerve is uncommon and 
typically causes tongue and auricular lesions or facial palsy and was described 
by Hunt in 1907.(1) The patient received IV acyclovir and oral prednisone with 
rapid improvement in pain and resolution of lesions and improvement of hearing 
over 1 month.
