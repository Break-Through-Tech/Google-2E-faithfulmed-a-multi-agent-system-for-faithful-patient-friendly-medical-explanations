Transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation as adjunct to primary care 
management for tennis elbow: pragmatic randomised controlled trial (TATE trial).

van der Windt DA(1).

STUDY QUESTION: Can transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation (TENS), as a 
patient controlled adjunct to primary care management for tennis elbow, provide 
superior pain relief to primary care management alone.
SUMMARY ANSWER: TENS conferred no additional clinical benefit over primary care 
management consisting of information and advice on analgesia and exercise for 
patients with tennis elbow, probably partly owing to poor adherence to treatment 
recommendations.
WHAT IS KNOWN AND WHAT THIS PAPER ADDS: A need exists for safe, self 
administered interventions to provide pain relief for patients with tennis 
elbow. TENS as an adjunct to primary care management failed to show any 
additional pain relief compared with primary care management alone.
