[Postoperative care after cataract surgery].

The postoperative treatment of cataract surgery is an important element for 
success of the surgery. The postoperative therapy primarily aims at the 
prevention of intraocular bacterial infection by administration of antibiotics. 
Anti-inflammatory drugs can suppress the operation-related inflammation. In 
addition to fundamental risks of topical eye drop application, this review 
article discusses the current therapeutic strategies for the prevention of 
postoperative bacterial infection and suppression of surgically induced 
inflammation.
